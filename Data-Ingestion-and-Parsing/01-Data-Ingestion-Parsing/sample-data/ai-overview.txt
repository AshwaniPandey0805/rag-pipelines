Artificial Intelligence and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a pattern that enhances Large Language Models (LLMs)
by dynamically fetching relevant external context from a knowledge base before generating responses.

Key benefits of RAG:
1. Prevents hallucinations by grounding LLM responses in verifiable facts.
2. Keeps knowledge up to date without costly retraining or fine-tuning.
3. Provides source attribution and citations for auditability.
